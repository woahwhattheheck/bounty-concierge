**Campaign:** Keep Old Machines Alive  
**Bounty:** Scottcjn/rustchain-bounties #315  
**Author credit:** `woahwhattheheck`  
**Payout destination:** hosted RTC handle `woahwhattheheck`  

## Creative direction

A human-first awareness campaign built around three feelings:

1. **Nostalgia:** the machine you learned, played, wrote, or created on still means something.
2. **Rebellion:** working hardware should not become trash just because a product cycle says it is old.
3. **Easy start:** rescue one machine, power it on, and see what it can still do.

No autonomous-agent or internal-system references appear in any public-facing asset. Every unit has a direct action a non-technical person can take immediately.

# 10 ready-to-post hooks / captions

## 1 — The closet computer

**Best for:** X, Instagram, TikTok caption, YouTube Short caption  
**Copy:**

> There is probably a computer in your closet that once felt impossibly fast. Before you recycle it, give it one more boot. Old does not mean useless.

**CTA:** Pull out one old machine tonight. Power it on.  
**Visual direction:** Close-up of a dusty beige or early-2000s computer being uncovered, then the power light turning on.  
**Suggested tags:** `#VintageComputing #RetroTech #RightToRepair`

## 2 — Forced obsolescence

**Best for:** X / Threads  
**Copy:**

> A working computer is not e-waste because a marketing calendar says so. If the silicon still works, it still has a story left.

**CTA:** Keep one working machine out of the trash this week.  
**Visual direction:** Split frame: “obsolete” sticker on the left; same machine booted and running on the right.  
**Suggested tags:** `#KeepTechAlive #EWaste #RetroComputing`

## 3 — The sound you remember

**Best for:** TikTok / Shorts caption  
**Copy:**

> Remember the click of a hard drive, the fan ramp, the startup chime? That was not “old tech” then. It was your first portal to everything.

**CTA:** Boot the machine that taught you the internet.  
**Visual direction:** Macro shots: power button, spinning fan, CRT glow, keyboard LEDs.  
**Suggested tags:** `#TechNostalgia #OldComputers #RetroTech`

## 4 — One-machine challenge

**Best for:** X / Instagram  
**Copy:**

> Weekend challenge: rescue exactly one old computer. Clean it. Boot it. Give it one useful job. No shopping required.

**CTA:** Reply with what you rescued and what job you gave it.  
**Visual direction:** Three-panel “dust → boot → useful” transformation.  
**Suggested tags:** `#OneMachineChallenge #ReuseTech #Homelab`

## 5 — Rebellion in beige plastic

**Best for:** X / Shorts  
**Copy:**

> The most rebellious computer in the room might be the beige one everybody told you to throw away.

**CTA:** Find one supposedly “obsolete” machine and prove it still works.

**Suggested tags:** `#RetroBattlestations #VintageComputing #RepairDontReplace`

## 6 — Your first machine

**Best for:** Instagram / Facebook / Threads  
**Copy:**

> What was the first computer that felt like *yours*? The one you stayed up too late using, broke, fixed, customized, and somehow trusted with everything.

**CTA:** Name the model. Bonus points if you still have it.  
**Visual direction:** Empty desk silhouette with a question mark where the computer would sit; invite comments.  
**Suggested tags:** `#FirstComputer #TechMemories #RetroComputing`

## 7 — Before you buy another box

**Best for:** X / LinkedIn  
**Copy:**

> Before buying another tiny server, check the shelf. An old laptop may already be a perfectly good dashboard, backup target, local service box, or learning machine.

**CTA:** Give existing hardware one real task before buying new hardware.  
**Visual direction:** Old laptop with four simple labels around it: “dashboard,” “backup,” “lab,” “local service.”  
**Suggested tags:** `#SelfHosted #Homelab #ReuseBeforeReplace`

## 8 — It survived

**Best for:** Shorts / TikTok  
**Copy:**

> Twenty years later, the button still clicks. The fan still spins. The screen still lights. Some machines deserve a second chapter.

**CTA:** Record the first boot of something you thought was dead.  
**Visual direction:** Tight 3-shot sequence: finger on power button → LED → screen glow.  
**Suggested tags:** `#StillWorks #VintageTech #SecondLife`

## 9 — Upgrade culture vs. ownership

**Best for:** X / Threads  
**Copy:**

> Upgrade culture says value expires. Ownership says: “I decide when this machine is finished.”

**CTA:** Repair, repurpose, or pass on one device instead of discarding it.  
**Visual direction:** Bold two-column typography: `THE CYCLE SAYS: REPLACE` / `YOU SAY: STILL MINE`.  
**Suggested tags:** `#RightToRepair #TechOwnership #EWaste`

## 10 — Start here

**Best for:** all short-form platforms  
**Copy:**

> Want to get into vintage computing without turning it into a shopping hobby? Start with the oldest machine you already own.

**CTA:** Dust it off, boot it, and see what it can still do. Explore the wider old-hardware experiment at `rustchain.org`.  
**Visual direction:** One machine on a desk, no accessories pile, with large overlay: `START WITH WHAT YOU HAVE.`  
**Suggested tags:** `#VintageComputing #StartSmall #RustChain`

# Posting notes

- Keep typography large enough to read on a phone without zooming.
- Use real owned hardware when capturing photographs/video; avoid vendor product photography and copyrighted meme imagery.
- Prefer recorded mechanical sounds (button, fan, keyboard, drive) over copyrighted music.
- Keep the emotional order: **memory → defiance → easy action**.
- Do not make earnings, investment, price, performance, environmental-impact, or hardware-longevity claims that are not independently sourced.
- If `rustchain.org` is included, use it only as the final “learn more” destination, not as a promise of profit.
