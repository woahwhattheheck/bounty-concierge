## Why

Unchecked addition can overflow a running total even when each individual amount fits its type. Existing broad arithmetic warnings do not identify whether a binding carries an accumulated value across loop iterations.

## Changes

- Add and register `loop_accumulator` with finding code `SANCT_LOOP_ACCUMULATOR`.
- Flag identifier targets updated with `+=` or self-addition in `for`, `while`, and `loop`, including repeated `while` conditions and nested loops.
- Track lexical bindings so fresh iteration variables and shadows stay clear; keep checked/saturating APIs and deferred closure/async bodies outside these unchecked patterns.
- Suggest explicit `checked_add` handling or intentional saturation, and document the syntactic analysis limits.
- Add one maintained golden snapshot covering six positive sites and the relevant negative controls, plus the detector catalog and error-code entries.
